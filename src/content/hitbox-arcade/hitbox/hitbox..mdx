## Overview

The Hit Box was the commercial product that made the all-button arcade layout legible to the wider fighting-game community.

It popularized the leverless form factor to the point that "Hit Box" or "Hitobx" is still used generically for the leverless category of controllers.

## Hardware

### Materials

**Case**: Steel with thick acrylic top panel

**Bottom Surface**: No-slip bottom material

**Cable**: Detachable braided USB cable

### Switch Configuration

**Stock Switch**: Sanwa OBSF-24 (24mm, directional and action buttons) + Sanwa OBSF-30 (30mm, jump button, red)

## Compatibility

- **Firmware**: Proprietary Hit Box firmware (Uses a Brook PCB)
- **Native support**: PS4, Nintendo Switch, PC
- **Connection**: Wired detachable USB cable
