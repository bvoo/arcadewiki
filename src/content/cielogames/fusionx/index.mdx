## Overview

FusionX is CIELOGAMES' sloped, lap-sized leverless, built around wrist support and a wider chassis.

It shares the company's low-profile Kasumi switch platform with the FusionEdge but uses a more aggressive ergonomic shell.

## Hardware

### Switch Configuration

**Stock Switch**: Gateron Low-Profile Kasumi Axis (霞軸)

### Electronics

- **PCB / Controller**: RP2040-based PCB built around a Raspberry Pi Pico W on the PC version; Brook P5 on the PS5 version
- **Firmware / Software**: GP2040-CE on the PC version; Brook firmware on the PS5 version
- **Special features**: Board options differ by platform target


### Physical Characteristics

The FusionX features a steep angled/sloped top panel and a magnetic removable bottom plate. The top panel uses triple-layer acrylic, chamfered by hand. Optional backlit LED with app control available.

## Compatibility

- **Firmware**: GP2040-CE on the PC version; Brook firmware on the PS5 version
- **Native support**: PC, PS4, Nintendo Switch, and Switch 2 on the PC version; those platforms plus PS5 on the PS5 version
- **Connection**: Wired USB
- **Notes**: The PS5 version uses the Brook board stack to extend console support beyond the standard RP2040-based PCB configuration.
