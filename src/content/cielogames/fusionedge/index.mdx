## Overview

FusionEdge is one of CIELOGAMES' flat-profile leverless, with an interesting RGB setup around the controller.

The design centers on a slim top surface and low-profile Kasumi switches rather than the more aggressively sloped ergonomics of the FusionX.

## Hardware

### Switch Configuration

**Stock Switch**: Gateron Low-Profile Kasumi Axis (霞軸)

### Electronics

- **PCB / Controller**: RP2040-based PCB built around a Raspberry Pi Pico W on the PC version; Brook P5 on the PS5 version; Brook Universal Fighting Board on the Brook version
- **Firmware / Software**: GP2040-CE on the PC version; Brook firmware on Brook-based variants

### Physical Characteristics

The FusionEdge uses a flat top panel (no wrist slope). Optional backlit LED version available.

## Compatibility

- **Firmware**: GP2040-CE on the PC version; Brook firmware on Brook-based variants
- **Native support**: PC, PS4, Nintendo Switch, and Android on the PC version; broader console support on Brook-equipped variants
- **Connection**: Wired USB
- **Notes**: The PS5 version adds PS5 support through the Brook P5, and the Brook version likely uses a P5 Plus (unconfirmed).
