## Overview

The Rana Tadpole is a pocket-sized digital controller built for Smash and other platform fighters.

Its design emphasizes portability, open-source repairability, and a compact aluminum shell rather than the larger desk-first form factor common to most rectangles.

## Highlights

- Extremely compact 180 × 115 mm footprint
- CNC-machined 6061 aluminum case with injection-molded PBT buttons
- Kailh GM 4.0 mouse switches for short-travel digital inputs
- Replaceable USB-C daughterboard with community-friendly firmware options

## Hardware

### Case and Materials

- **Case**: CNC-machined 6061 aluminum with anodized finish
- **Buttons / Keycaps**: Injection-molded PBT buttons
- **Bottom**: Grip pads made of textured silicone rubber
- **Mounting**: Open-source internal design with replaceable daughterboard hardware

### Switches

- **Stock switch**: Kailh GM 4.0 Mouseclick

### Electronics

- **PCB / Controller**: RP2040-based PCB with a replaceable USB-C daughterboard
- **Special features**: Open-source design files and user documentation are available

## Compatibility

- **Firmware**: HayBox
- **Connection**: Wired USB-C via a replaceable daughterboard
