## Overview

The Razer Kitsune helped push leverless controllers into the mainstream by combining a licensed PS5 package with the fit, finish, and industrial design of a premium consumer peripheral.

Its low-profile optical switches and thin chassis make it one of the most travel-friendly commercial options in the category.

## Hardware

### Materials

**Case**: Aluminum shell

**Bottom Surface**: Full rubber non-slip bottom

### Switch Configuration

**Stock Switch**: Razer Low-Profile Linear Optical switches

### Additional Features

- 4 SOCD cleaning modes (added via firmware 2.0 update): Neutral (default), First Input Priority, Last Input Priority, Neither
- Tournament lock switch (disables non-essential buttons)
- Razer Chroma RGB around edges

## Compatibility

- **Firmware**: Proprietary Razer firmware
- **Native support**: PS5 and PC (Windows)
- **Connection**: Wired USB-C to USB-A
- **Notes**: Official PS5 licensing provides native support on PS5. PS4, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch are not natively supported. Firmware 2.0 added additional SOCD modes.
