## Overview

The Superslab is Prong's full-size platform-fighter slab, built around a wider 20-button layout and direct GameCube-oriented support.

Compared with the smaller Jabslab, it is closer to a tournament-first Smash rectangle: broader on the lap, heavier in hand, and shipped with both USB and GameCube cabling.

## Highlights

- Full-size layout
- 20 Kailh Choc Red Pro low-profile switches with polycarbonate caps
- Browser-based configuration, firmware updates, and LED customization

## Hardware

### Case and Materials

- **Case**: Aluminum chassis
- **Faceplate options**: Acrylic or anodized aluminum top layer, depending on configuration
- **Buttons / Keycaps**: Polycarbonate caps on the main inputs; 6 silicone rubber option buttons
- **Accessories fit**: Includes a controller sleeve, bottom grip pad, foam key dampeners, and hex keys for disassembly

### Switches

- **Stock switch**: Kailh Choc Red Pro
- **Type**: Low-profile linear mechanical switch
- **Adjustability**: Main settings, profiles, and lighting are configurable through the web interface

### Electronics

- **PCB / Controller**: Proprietary Prong controller electronics
- **Firmware / Software**: Proprietary firmware with a browser-based settings and update page
- **Special features**: 1000 Hz polling, gyroscopic motion controls, high-frequency per-button LEDs, and profile-based remapping

## Compatibility

- **Firmware**: Proprietary Prong firmware with web-based configuration and firmware updating
- **Native support**: GameCube, Wii, Nintendo Switch, PS3, PS4, and PC
- **Connection**: Wired USB-C, with included USB-C to USB-A and USB-C to GameCube cables
- **Notes**: The storefront lists GameCube adapter, DInput, XInput, and keyboard-oriented USB modes. Switch support is described as Fightpad or GameCube-adapter based, depending on setup.
