## Overview

The Varmilo FK2 is one of the few commercial leverless controllers built around true analog key sensing instead of conventional digital switches.

Its Cherry MX Multipoint Silver switches report position data rather than simple on / off states, allowing analog-style inputs from a button layout without a traditional joystick.

## Hardware

### Materials

**Case**: ABS

**Button Caps**: ABS keycaps

**Bottom Surface**: Anti-slip bottom

### Switch Configuration

**Stock Switch**: Cherry MX Multipoint Silver (analog inductive / Hall Effect)

### Electronics

- **PCB / Controller**: Proprietary Varmilo controller electronics
- **Firmware / Software**: Proprietary Varmilo firmware with a web-based configurator at fk2.varmilo.com
- **Special features**: Configurable SOCD cleaning and adjustable simultaneous-press behavior for Up+Down and Left+Right independently

## Compatibility

- **Firmware**: Proprietary Varmilo firmware
- **Native support**: PC (Windows)
- **Connection**: Wired USB-C to USB-A
- **Notes**: Console support depends on separate dongles for PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch. Polling rate is 1,000 Hz with a quoted response speed of 5 ms or less.
