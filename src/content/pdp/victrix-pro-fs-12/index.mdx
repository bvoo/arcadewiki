## Overview

The Victrix Pro FS 12 is Victrix's flagship all-metal leverless, built around a single-piece aluminum chassis and tournament-oriented fit and finish.

## Hardware

### Materials

**Case**: Aluminum single-piece chassis (brushed/anodized/illuminated finish)

**Base**: Foam lap pad

### Switch Configuration

**Stock Switch**: Sanwa Denshi OBSF-24 buttons

- Button sizes: 8x 24mm action buttons + directional buttons; 1x 30mm jump/up button
- Total: 12 Sanwa Denshi buttons (leverless layout)


### Physical Characteristics

The Pro FS 12 is the heaviest controller in its class at 3,538g (7.8 lbs), owing to its all-aluminum construction. It features a 6.28-degree integrated wrist slope, built-in carrying handles, and cable organizers.

### Additional Features

- Quick-access back panel for modifications
- Integrated carrying handles
- Cable organizers
- Tournament mode lock
- 3.5mm audio jack
- PS5/PS4/PC mode switch
- Control bar with PS touchpad

## Compatibility

- **Firmware**: Proprietary Victrix firmware and control electronics
- **Native support**: PS5, PS4, PC
- **Connection**: Wired detachable USB-C to USB-A
- **Notes**: Official PlayStation licensing provides native console support without the adapter-based exceptions common on RP2040-based boards. Input latency is rated at about 5 ms.
