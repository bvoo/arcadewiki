## Overview

The 8BitDo All-Button Arcade Controller brings the company's mass-market controller design approach to a slim leverless format.

It targets players who want hot-swappable low-profile switches and console-specific SKUs at a lower price tier than most licensed leverless options.

## Hardware

### Materials

**Top Panel**: Tempered glass (fingerprint-proof)

**Bottom Surface**: Silicone non-slip pad

**Feet**: Magnetic foldable metal feet

### Switch Configuration

**Stock Switch**: Kailh Wizard low-profile mechanical switches (16 total)

Hot-swappable PCB - compatible with other Kailh Choc v2 switches.

## Compatibility

- **Firmware**: Proprietary 8BitDo firmware configurable through 8BitDo Ultimate Software V2
- **Native support**: Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and Windows on the standard model; Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One, and Windows on the Xbox model
- **Connection**: Bluetooth + 2.4G + wired USB-C on the Switch/Windows model; 2.4G + wired USB-C on the Xbox model
- **Notes**: Wired latency is rated at 4 ms. The Switch/Windows model uses a 1,000 mAh battery rated for about 20 hours. The Xbox model uses a 3,000 mAh battery rated for about 20 hours with RGB or 40 hours without RGB.
