## Overview

GRAM FGC is an ultra-slim leverless for traditional fighting games built around low-profile Choc switches and an open-source RP2040-based PCB.

It focused on portability, and repairability over mainstream console-focused features.

## Highlights

- 11mm chassis using a floating switch design
- Kailh Choc V1 low-profile switch platform with PG1350 hot-swap sockets
- RP2040-based board with a custom GP2040-CE fork
- Lightweight build with powder-coated aluminum panels and a neoprene travel case

## Hardware

### Case and Materials

- **Frame**: SLA resin
- **Panels**: Powder-coated aluminum
- **Buttons / Keycaps**: PBT 24mm Choc buttons
- **Bottom**: Rubberized backing
- **Mounting**: Screwless front panels with a floating switch design

### Switches

- **Stock switch**: Kailh Choc Red Pro
- **Type**: Linear, low-profile mechanical
- **Hot-swappable**: Yes
- **Adjustability**: Uses PG1350 hot-swap sockets and supports Kailh Choc V1 switches

### Electronics

- **PCB / Controller**: RP2040-based PCB designed by Quark.Works and GRAM

## Compatibility

- **Firmware**: GP2040-CE (custom fork with PS4 support)
- **Native support**: PC, PS3, PS4, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch
- **Connection**: Wired USB-C
- **Notes**: Compatibility is largely defined by the GP2040-CE firmware stack. Sold with USB-C to USB-A and USB-C to GameCube cables.

## In the Box

- GRAM FGC controller
- GRAM neoprene soft case
- Braided USB-C to GameCube cable
- Braided USB-C to USB-A cable

## Notes

- The GRAM FGC shares its slim construction approach with the GRAM Slim, but targets traditional fighting games instead of platform fighters.
